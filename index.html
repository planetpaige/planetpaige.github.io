About Me!

I'm a 4th year Informatics major at UC Irvine specializing in HCI. I love learning about user experience design and web development, and I'm particularly interested in creating accessible and inclusive digital experiences.

Researching with Professor Stacy Branham and Professor Jennifer Wong-ma at INsite Lab and working as a Digital Accessibility Supervisor with DASH (Digital Accessibility Support and Help) has given me plenty of opportunities to learn about WCAG principles, ADA regulations, and inclusive design practices.

Outside of academics, I enjoy musical theatre, collecting Legos and Peach Riot figures, and traveling with friends and family. I have spent a lot of time exploring new interests through my positions in Hack at UCI and VenusHacks as well as various radio experiences with KUCI.
