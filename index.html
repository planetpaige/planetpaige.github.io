About Me!

I'm a 4th year Informatics major at UC Irvine specializing in HCI. I love learning about user experience design and web development, and I'm particularly interested in creating accessible and inclusive digital experiences.

Researching with Professor Stacy Branham and Professor Jennifer Wong-ma at INsite Lab and previously working with DASH (Digital Accessibility Support and Help) has given me plenty of opportunities to learn about WCAG principles, ADA regulations, and inclusive design practices. I'm currently a grader for Professor André van der Hoek's SWE 121 course, where I review and critique assignments to help students with application and interaction design.

Outside of academics, I enjoy musical theatre, collecting Legos and Peach Riot figures, and traveling with friends and family. I have spent a lot of time exploring new interests through my positions in Hack at UCI and VenusHacks as well as various radio experiences with KUCI.

In Progress

MUGIC Games+

UX Design | UCI Informatics Capstone | Oct 2026 - Present

A two-player cooperative cooking game controlled entirely through MUGIC motion sensors, designed so older adults, young children, and players with motor or vision disabilities can play together. Players split roles as the head and feet of a goblin in a trench coat, communicating across a divided kitchen to complete recipes through motion-based minigames, with adjustable sensitivity, audio and visual cues, and accessibility settings built in.

Clarity Coach

UX Research and Design | UCI INsite Lab | Aug 2025 - Present

A professor-facing accessibility tool that detects and flags inaccessible language in real time, helping instructors communicate more clearly and supporting blind and remote students through more inclusive lecture delivery.
